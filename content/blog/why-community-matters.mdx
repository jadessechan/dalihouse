<SectionHead label="Reality" id="loneliness-curve">
  The loneliness curve is real.
</SectionHead>

<Columns>
  <p>Research consistently shows that the hardest part of relocating is the social gap, not the moving logistics. Most people underestimate how long it takes to build meaningful friendships in a new city. The average? <strong>Six months to a year</strong> before you feel like you truly "belong."</p>

  <p>That's a long time to eat dinner alone.</p>

  <p>When you move into a solo apartment, your social life starts from zero. You go to work, come home, and the cycle repeats. Weekend plans require effort: finding events, showing up alone, hoping to click with someone you've never met.</p>

  <p>Random roommate situations aren't much better. You might get lucky, or you might end up with someone whose lifestyle clashes with yours. There's no curation, no shared values, no intention behind it.</p>
</Columns>
