<SectionHead label="The Map" id="metroplex-size">
  The metroplex is bigger than you think.
</SectionHead>

<Columns>
  <p>DFW is a sprawling metro of dozens of cities and suburbs. Dallas, Fort Worth, Plano, Carrollton, Frisco, Arlington, Richardson all blend together, and where you live determines your experience more than you'd expect.</p>

  <p>If you're working downtown, living in Plano means a 30–40 minute commute. If you're remote, a quieter suburb might be perfect. Know your priorities before picking a neighborhood. (For a women-focused breakdown of each area, see <a href="/blog/relocating-to-dallas-as-a-woman">Relocating to Dallas as a Woman</a>.)</p>
</Columns>

<SectionHead label="Getting Around" labelColor="tan" headingColor="brown" id="need-a-car">
  You'll probably need a car.
</SectionHead>

<SingleCol>
  <p>Public transit exists (DART rail and buses), but DFW is a car city. Most people drive, and distances between things can be surprising. Budget for a car payment, insurance, and gas, or choose a central location where you can walk or rideshare to essentials.</p>
</SingleCol>

<SectionHead label="The Numbers" id="cost-of-living">
  Cost of living is lower than coastal cities, but rising.
</SectionHead>

<SingleCol>
  <p>Compared to New York, LA, or San Francisco, Dallas is affordable. But rents have climbed significantly in the last few years, especially in popular neighborhoods. A one-bedroom apartment in a nice area runs $1,200–$1,800/month.</p>

  <p>Coliving options like Dali House at $900/month with utilities included can stretch your budget much further, especially in your first year, when you're still figuring out which part of the metroplex you actually want to live in long-term.</p>
</SingleCol>

<SectionHead label="The Food" labelColor="tan" headingColor="brown" id="food-scene">
  The food scene is seriously underrated.
</SectionHead>

<SingleCol>
  <p>Dallas has incredible Tex-Mex (obviously), but also world-class Korean, Vietnamese, Ethiopian, and barbecue.</p>

  <ul>
    <li><strong>Deep Ellum</strong>: dining and nightlife in one walkable district.</li>
    <li><strong>Bishop Arts</strong>: independent restaurants and boutiques south of downtown.</li>
    <li><strong>Carrollton Koreatown</strong>: a hidden gem it takes most newcomers months to find.</li>
  </ul>
</SingleCol>
