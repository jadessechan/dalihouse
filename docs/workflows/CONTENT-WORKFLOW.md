# CONTENT-WORKFLOW.md - Dali House Content Workflow

_Status: working MVP workflow. This is designed to help ship consistent blog and social content before the full brand system is finalized._

## Objective

Use content to do three things:
- attract the right future residents
- build trust and clarity around what Dali House is
- turn each blog post into reusable social content

## Primary content strategy

Dali House content should sit at the intersection of:
- Dallas relocation help
- women-centered coliving
- intentional community
- soft landing / life transition support
- practical lifestyle guidance

## Core content pillars

### 1. Relocating to Dallas
Examples:
- moving to Dallas guides
- best neighborhoods for young professional women
- what Dallas actually costs
- how to make friends in Dallas

### 2. Coliving explained
Examples:
- what coliving is
- how coliving compares to apartments or random roommates
- why coliving works for relocation
- who coliving is right for

### 3. Community and belonging
Examples:
- why community matters in a new city
- loneliness after relocation
- how intentional house culture changes the experience
- balancing privacy with connection

### 4. The Dali House experience
Examples:
- amenities and what’s included
- furnished living and flexibility
- what the application process is like
- how residents are selected

### 5. Founder perspective
Examples:
- Jadesse’s story
- moving back to Dallas
- why Dali House exists
- what “soft landing” means in real life

## Blog workflow

### Step 1: Choose the target topic
Start with one of the core pillars.

Ask:
- What question is the audience already searching or wondering about?
- What emotional pain point is underneath it?
- How can Dali House offer a useful and specific perspective?

### Step 2: Define the angle
Every post should have:
- a clear search or reader intent
- a Dali House angle
- one core takeaway

### Step 3: Create the outline
Recommended structure:
1. strong opening hook
2. frame the real problem
3. give practical insight or guidance
4. connect back to Dali House naturally
5. close with a calm CTA

### Step 4: Draft the article
Writing rules:
- lead with empathy and specificity
- use practical language
- avoid fluff
- prefer examples, contrasts, and clear sections
- keep paragraphs short and skimmable

### Step 5: Edit for tone
The post should feel:
- informed
- warm
- trustworthy
- helpful
- grounded in real life

### Step 6: SEO pass
Every post should include:
- one clear target keyword/topic
- a strong title
- a meta description
- meaningful headings
- internal links when available
- a relevant CTA to Dali House

### Step 7: Publish and repurpose
Each blog post should generate:
- 1 Instagram carousel
- 1 Instagram caption
- 3 story frames or story prompts
- optional quote card or short reel concept

## Blog quality bar

A Dali House blog post should:
- answer a real question
- be useful even if the reader never converts
- sound like a smart, calm guide
- reflect the Dali House worldview
- make Dali House feel like the natural next step, not a forced pitch

## Preferred blog anatomy

Use this shape by default:
- Hook
- Problem or context
- 3 to 5 practical points
- Dali House perspective
- CTA

## CTA pattern

Use natural, relevant CTAs such as:
- Apply to Dali House
- Tour the space
- See if Dali House is the right fit
- Start your soft landing in Dallas

## Repurposing rules

When converting blog -> social:
- do not copy-paste the entire article
- pull out the strongest argument or most useful list
- use a single clean idea per carousel
- preserve the same tone and emotional framing
- make the content mobile-readable and scroll-friendly

## Suggested weekly rhythm

MVP cadence:
- 1 blog post per week or every 2 weeks
- 1 primary carousel per blog post
- 1 to 3 supporting stories or lightweight social posts

## Instruction for future agents

When creating content for Dali House, use the content pillars above. Prioritize Dallas relocation, women-centered coliving, community, and soft landing themes. Make each post useful first, then brand-aligned, then conversion-aware. Every substantial blog post should be repurposable into at least one Instagram carousel.
