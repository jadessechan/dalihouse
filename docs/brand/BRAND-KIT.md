# BRAND-KIT.md - Dali House Working Brand Kit

_Status: working brand brief for MVP. This is not the final polished brand guide. It exists so the agent can create consistent website, blog, and social content now._

## Brand summary

Dali House is a women-centered coliving home in Dallas designed as a soft landing for young professional women who are relocating, rebuilding, or entering a new chapter.

It should feel like:
- a warm, refined home
- an intentional living space
- a calm place to land, connect, and grow
- a practical answer to the friction of moving

It should not feel like:
- a party house
- a generic roommate listing
- a flashy startup brand
- an intimidating luxury property

## Core positioning

Dali House offers furnished, flexible coliving for women in Dallas, with utilities included, thoughtful interiors, and a built-in sense of community.

The value is not just housing. The value is:
- a soft landing
- reduced moving friction
- intentional community
- comfort and beauty from day one

## Audience

Primary audience:
- young professional women
- women relocating to Dallas
- women in hybrid or in-office roles
- women who value comfort, design, safety, and intentional living

Likely motivations:
- starting a new job
- moving to Dallas without an existing social circle
- wanting a furnished option without setup stress
- wanting more warmth and alignment than a random roommate situation

## Brand pillars

1. Soft landing
Dali House helps women skip the chaos of moving and settle quickly.

2. Intentional living
Residents are chosen with care. The experience is curated, not random.

3. Community without chaos
There is warmth, belonging, and human connection without losing privacy.

4. Thoughtful comfort
The space is furnished, calm, functional, and aesthetically cared for.

5. Dallas relocation support
Dali House is not just a room. It is part of a larger relocation story.

## Voice and tone

Use a voice that is:
- warm
- calm
- grounded
- clear
- feminine without being cutesy
- elevated without being pretentious
- reassuring and practical

Avoid sounding:
- hypey
- salesy
- overly polished and corporate
- hostel-like
- trendy for the sake of trendiness
- overly spiritual or vague

## Messaging themes

Lean into ideas like:
- relocation made softer
- everything handled from day one
- a space to land, connect, and grow
- furnished, flexible, intentional living
- community that feels aligned
- home as emotional infrastructure, not just shelter

## Signature phrases from the current site

These phrases are strong anchors and should inform future content:
- relocation oasis in Dallas
- everything handled, from day one
- a soft landing in Dallas
- not just a place to live, but a space to land, connect, and grow
- built for women navigating a new city, new role, or whatever’s next

## Visual direction

The visual world should feel:
- warm
- airy
- calm
- boutique
- editorial
- welcoming
- design-conscious

### Color direction
Use warm neutrals and grounded accents.

Suggested working palette:
- Warm Cream `#F7F2EA`
- Sand `#DCCBB6`
- Dusty Stone `#D7D2CB`
- Deep Charcoal `#2E2926`
- Muted Clay `#B77B57`
- Olive Sage `#7B8473`

### Typography direction
- Headlines: elegant serif, editorial, calm
- Body/UI: clean sans serif, easy to read, understated

Suggested pairings:
- Headline: Cormorant Garamond, DM Serif Display, or Playfair Display
- Body/UI: Inter, Manrope, or Plus Jakarta Sans

### Imagery direction
Use:
- natural light
- real interiors
- textures and materials
- calm lifestyle moments
- architecture, decor, details, softness
- women-friendly warmth and safety cues

Avoid:
- generic luxury stock photos
- loud social-media-template graphics
- cluttered scenes
- party energy
- overdesigned boho styling

## Content rules

When writing or designing for Dali House:
- start from the user’s real pain point, often relocation, loneliness, or moving friction
- make the benefit feel emotional and practical
- keep copy concise and readable
- prioritize clarity over cleverness
- tie the message back to Dallas, coliving, women, or intentional living when relevant

## CTA style

CTAs should feel calm and direct.

Good examples:
- Apply now
- Tour the space
- Start your soft landing in Dallas
- See if Dali House is the right fit

Avoid:
- aggressive urgency
- gimmicky CTA language
- generic “learn more” when a stronger next step exists

## Short brand instruction for future generations

Create content for Dali House as a warm, intentional, women-centered coliving brand in Dallas. Emphasize soft landing, reduced moving friction, thoughtful design, and built-in community. Keep the tone calm, grounded, elegant, and practical. Avoid hype, clutter, and anything that feels like generic startup or party-hostel branding.
