# SOCIALS.md - Dali House Social Media System

_Status: working system for MVP launch. This exists so the agent can create Instagram content consistently even before the final brand guide is complete._

## Primary platform

Current priority platform:
- Instagram

Purpose of social content:
- build trust
- make Dali House feel real and emotionally legible
- turn blog insights into shareable, saveable content
- create consistency between the website, blog, and socials

## Social positioning

Dali House social content should feel like:
- a calm, design-conscious hospitality/lifestyle brand
- a trustworthy guide for women relocating to Dallas
- a thoughtful founder-led brand with real perspective

It should not feel like:
- influencer content spam
- generic motivational quote content
- loud engagement bait
- trendy content detached from the actual offering

## Main social content buckets

### 1. Educational carousels
Examples:
- what to know before moving to Dallas
- what coliving actually is
- how to make friends in a new city
- why intentional living matters

### 2. Founder perspective
Examples:
- why I created Dali House
- what I wish I knew when I moved back to Dallas
- the emotional side of relocating

### 3. House and experience
Examples:
- what’s included
- room details
- amenities
- application process
- what makes Dali House different

### 4. Community and culture
Examples:
- who Dali House is for
- the difference between curated community and random roommates
- home as a space to land and grow

## Instagram carousel default structure

Preferred sequence:
1. Hook
2. Context or tension
3. Core idea
4. Supporting detail or list
5. Dali House angle or takeaway
6. CTA

## Carousel writing rules

- Keep slides spacious
- Use short headlines
- Keep body text concise
- Prefer one idea per slide
- Write for mobile readability first
- Use emotional clarity, not hype
- Avoid text-heavy walls

## Visual rules

Every carousel should feel:
- warm
- airy
- editorial
- calm
- clean
- grounded

Use:
- warm neutrals
- elegant typography
- clear hierarchy
- generous whitespace
- natural imagery or subtle visual texture

Avoid:
- loud gradients
- busy templates
- meme aesthetics
- clickbait design
- random visual styles from post to post

## Caption rules

Captions should:
- extend the idea from the carousel, not repeat it exactly
- feel warm and useful
- include a clear but calm CTA
- avoid sounding overly optimized or spammy

Good CTA examples:
- Would you want this kind of soft landing?
- Thinking about moving to Dallas?
- If this sounds like your next chapter, apply to Dali House.

## Blog -> Instagram repurposing system

For each blog post, generate:
- 1 main educational carousel
- 1 caption
- 3 short story frames or prompts
- optional reel idea or quote card if the topic supports it

Conversion rule:
- one blog post should become one clear social story arc, not a messy summary of everything

## Default one-shot output format

When asked to create a Dali House Instagram asset from a blog or idea, output:
1. post angle
2. hook options
3. slide-by-slide copy
4. caption
5. CTA
6. optional visual direction notes

## Prompt instruction for future generations

Create Instagram content for Dali House that feels calm, elegant, warm, and trustworthy. Focus on women relocating to Dallas, coliving, intentional community, and soft landing themes. Use editorial-style carousel structure, strong hooks, concise slide copy, and visually consistent brand direction. The content should feel helpful and beautiful, not loud or trend-chasing.
