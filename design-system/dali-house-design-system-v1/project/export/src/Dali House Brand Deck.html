Part I · Voice & Tone No. 03

How we sound

Warm, calm,
grounded.

Feminine without being cutesy. Elevated without pretension. Clarity over cleverness — always anchored to a real pain point.

Reach for

  • Clarity over cleverness
  • Emotional truth
  • Practical reassurance
  • A soft landing
  • "We handle the friction."

Avoid

  • Hype & startup energy
  • Party-hostel vibes
  • Generic luxury language
  • Exaggeration, filler copy
  • "Game-changing community."

Signature phrases — reuse, don't paraphrase

"a soft landing in Dallas" "your relocation oasis" "everything handled, from day one" "a space to land, connect & grow" "coliving for women in Dallas"

Part II · Color No. 06 — Surfaces

A page
has two moods, max.

Surfaces alternate down a page to create rhythm — never more than two adjacent. Solid color and photography only; no gradients, no patterns.

Light Cream
surface
The default About, Audience, FAQ, Host. Brown-deep type on cream. Calm, editorial, low contrast. Body copy lives here.
Warm Cream-light
surface
For rhythm Features, blog cover band. A half-step warmer than cream. Used to separate two cream sections without going dark.
Dark Charcoal
surface
For weight Hero, The Space, How It Works, Footer. Cream type on charcoal. Where the brand turns intimate. Tan eyebrows replace crimson.

Part III · The Process No. 13 — Application
An easy first step

The application

Apply in three
soft steps.

Not a funnel. A short, intentional conversation — so we can build the right room, with the right people, in the right rhythm.

01 Step one

Get to know you.

Share a little about your lifestyle, your work, your what's-next. We read every word — Jadesse personally.

— Takes 6 minutes, give or take.

02 Step two

Tour the space.

Drop by, or hop on a video call. Coffee's on, the cats are nosy, and you'll see exactly which room would be yours.

— In-person or virtual.

03 Step three

Make it home.

Screening, lease, deposit — handled quickly and gently. Then we'll see you on move-in day with the welcome bag.

— Most rooms confirmed in under a week.
