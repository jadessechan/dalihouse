Renting a room is mostly about the room. But a coliving home is about what's around it — the kitchen you stand in at 8am, the couch where the conversations actually happen, the bathroom that feels like your own. Every space at Dali House is furnished, considered, and built to be lived in. Here's a walk through, one doorway at a time.

01 Bedrooms

Bedroom One

North-facing · warm light

The first bedroom is the larger of the two, sitting at the front of the house with morning light through linen curtains. Full-size bed, a proper writing desk (not a folding table, not a corner of the kitchen), full-length mirror, and original artwork on the wall.

Everything is furnished. Bring your suitcase, your books, and whatever plant you can't bear to leave behind. The room is yours to make small adjustments to — Jadesse would rather you feel at home than feel like a tenant.

Bedroom 01 · Includes

  • Full-size bed, work station, TV
  • Full-length mirror, closet space
  • Original artwork on every wall
02 Bedrooms

Bedroom Two

South-facing · evening light

Bedroom two is a touch smaller and a touch quieter — set back from the street, with afternoon sun. Same setup as bedroom one: full bed, work station, TV, mirror, closet, art. The two rooms share a bath and a hallway that you'll probably linger in talking to your housemate.

Residents who want a deeper retreat after work tend to pick this room. Residents who like being in the middle of everything tend to pick the other. Both are good answers.

Bedroom 02 · Includes

  • Full-size bed, work station, TV
  • Quieter side of the house
  • Afternoon light · closet space
03 Shared Space

The Living Room

Vintage pieces · original artwork

The living room is the heart of the house and where most of the residency actually happens. Vintage furniture, original artwork on the walls, soft lighting at night, a record player and a stack of books.

This is the room where the work week thaws out — Sunday-night dinners, Wednesday evenings with a glass of wine, the long catch-up calls home. It's shared without ever feeling like a co-working lobby.

04 Shared Space

The Kitchen

Fully stocked · meal-prep ready

Fully stocked with the essentials — quality knives, sheet pans, a real coffee setup, mixing bowls in three sizes — so you can land on Sunday and have a meal cooked by Monday morning. No "we should buy a colander" texts.

Cabinet space is divided so each resident has her own pantry and fridge shelf. The communal pantry holds shared basics. Cooking is one of the easiest ways to feel at home — the kitchen is set up to make that frictionless.

05 Shared Space

The Shared Bath

Rain head · marble · bidet

The shared bathroom is the room residents are quietly the proudest of. Marble detailing, a rain showerhead, a handheld for the days you need it, and a bidet — a small luxury that surprises you the first morning and then you wonder how you lived without it.

Cleaning is monthly and handled. You'll never have a conversation about the shower drain.

06 Utility

Laundry

In-home · all hours

In-home washer and dryer. No coins, no app, no driving across the parking lot at 9pm to fold someone else's towels.

Small detail; large quality-of-life upgrade. Most residents say it's the one thing they didn't know to look for until they had it.
